Mango9 brings your organization’s business communications into one app.

Use your existing Mango9 account to:

• Make and receive business calls
• Access your assigned CRM records
• Send and receive SMS conversations
• Collaborate through tenant-scoped Team Chat
• Reply to connected social conversations when supported by the provider
• Support website visitors through Live Chat with assignment, visitor details and supported attachments
• Manage multiple business lines
• Open customer records directly from call and message activity
• Manage CRM appointments with calendar and list views, status updates, sharing and assignment
• Customize in-app appointment reminders and messaging notification preferences
• Use Assistant AI text and voice chat on eligible account-owner accounts, with supported file attachments, chat history and AI-assisted configuration review
• Connect your own supported ChatGPT account through OpenAI Codex, or Claude account through Anthropic Claude Code, using your organization’s CRM and authorized desktop setup to assist with CRM tasks
• Review supported workflow and voice-agent drafts in the connected CRM

Sign in with your Mango9 password or request a secure code by email. Your organization’s assigned services and calling line are provisioned automatically after authentication.

Mango9 is a companion app for existing Mango9 business customers. Accounts and telecommunications services are created and managed outside the app under organizational service agreements. Features depend on your organization’s services, account role and connected providers. Assistant requires an enabled account-owner account and your consent before sharing with AI services. Connected-computer replies require that computer and its AI tool to remain available; provider account limits apply. Optional manual SIP setup is available from Connection settings on the sign-in screen.
