• SMS, Team and Social messages are together in Conversations, with more consistent composers and attachment controls.
• Touch and hold a message for reactions, copy, reply, selection and forwarding, where supported. SMS reactions are Mango9-only and do not send an SMS or a tapback to the recipient.
• Delete messages and conversations for yourself across devices, and mute supported conversations without losing incoming messages.
• Social conversations include platform and account filters, attachment previews and clearer message dates.
• Open Calendar directly, or use Events & Tasks to switch between appointments, events and tasks.
• New notification controls for social messages, new leads, bulk-import summaries and appointment bookings. Supported alerts open the related item.
• Improved account-scoped refresh, saved-history reconciliation and messaging reliability.
