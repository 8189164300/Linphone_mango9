Mango9 is a companion app for existing Mango9 business customers. Accounts and telecommunications services are provisioned outside the app. There is no consumer signup, purchase, pricing or subscription link. Use the review credentials supplied separately in App Store Connect. Version 6.2.22 (67) supports iOS 15 and later.

This update improves Supervisor for eligible, enabled client-owner accounts. Open Conversations and tap the round AI button in the top header. The server verifies the signed-in owner and account scope; other roles cannot access owner-only operations.

Before chat, voice or applying AI-assisted settings, a compact AI privacy sheet explains data sent to OpenRouter and the configured chat provider (DeepInfra/DeepSeek or OpenAI), and to Google Gemini for voice. The user can decline or accept. Supervisor options > AI privacy lets the user review or revoke that choice. Revocation stops voice and cancels in-flight AI requests. Declining keeps AI chat and voice disabled.

Opening Voice does not connect or activate the microphone. Tap the central microphone/Connect control to begin. Mute pauses microphone input; End voice stops the session. Voice uses native echo cancellation and respects active phone calls, interruptions and backgrounding. It never reconnects automatically after an interruption. No purchase or billing links appear in the app.

Supervisor opens at the latest messages. History supports title search, text/voice filters and swipe-to-delete. Proposed settings appear as readable cards inside chat with Change and Apply controls. Applying requires the current reviewed settings and AI consent. Successful creations include a link to the created item. Device-protected, account-scoped local copies retain messages and settings summaries; saved settings must refresh before applying. Unconfirmed requests are checked against saved history, never blindly resent.

Other review steps:
1. Conversations contains SMS, Team Chat, Social and Live Chat. Settings > Conversations controls device-local tab visibility; server permissions still apply.
2. Tap + > Templates in a supported chat. Create and save a personal template, then Insert or Edit. Insert preserves the current draft and never sends automatically.
3. Live Chat supports website/status filters, assignment, visitor details and supported media. Social replies depend on connected-provider permissions. Unsupported social outgoing voice is not offered.
4. Tap + for supported images/files and the microphone for supported recordings. Native previews offer sharing/export. Long-press a message for supported copy, select, forward, delete-for-me and reaction actions. Message notifications open their matching chat.
5. CRM > Calendar and Events & Tasks display assigned records. Calling uses the assigned business line. Voice-message playback uses the loudspeaker by default and respects headphones and calls.
6. Social participant names open verified public profiles when a provider handle is available. Team, Social and Live Chat have a latest-message button while reading older messages.
7. Sign in normally with the supplied account credentials. The sign-in gear opens optional manual connection settings; its access code is the app version, 6.2.22. Normal account sign-in needs no manual server entry.
