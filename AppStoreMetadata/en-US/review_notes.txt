Mango9 6.2.27 (80): business communications and CRM companion, iOS 15+. Replacement for rejected build 79.

Clarification of our earlier reply: the final build retains manual SIP and removes only its extra settings-password gate.

Manual SIP is a fallback for connecting the phone app when the CRM is unavailable or Mango9 email sign-in cannot automatically provision the assigned calling line. Users open the gear on the sign-in screen and enter the Extension (SIP username), SIP password and server details supplied by their Mango9 administrator or service provider. Tap Register to connect the calling line. CRM access still requires normal Mango9 account sign-in.

Previously, this setup had a separate version-based settings password. Its only purpose was to reserve manual SIP configuration for Mango9 users, instead of presenting it as public general-purpose SIP setup. It was not intended to conceal functionality from App Review; the location and settings code were disclosed in the previous notes. To eliminate this restriction and address the possible review concern, build 80 removes that settings password and version check. Manual SIP remains fully available, opens directly from the gear, and still requires the user's actual SIP credentials. No extra settings code is needed.

Build 80 includes the latest build 79 fixes, white email-code labels, and white-and-blue manual SIP setup. Forgot password? opens a native email form that requests a reset link from Central; it does not open a browser or reset SIP credentials. Existing password/email-code sign-in and calling accounts are retained.

This build retains the compact chat-list selection controls introduced in build 79 for SMS, Team Chat, Social and Live Chat. Open Chat, choose a channel and tap the checkmark to select chats. The toolbar offers Select all, Mark read, Mark unread and Delete. Delete removes saved chat history only for the signed-in user; other participants keep their messages, and a new message can bring the chat back. Mark unread is a personal reminder, not a change to another participant's read receipt. New arrivals are not automatically selected. Failed actions remain selected for retry.

This build retains the Social Chat attachment, shared-card and profile-link improvements approved in version 6.2.26.

As in approved version 6.2.25, the app includes native CRM Opportunities, including values, stages, details, activity links, account-scoped filters and new-item alerts. Open CRM > Opportunities, or open a Lead or Client and use its Opportunity actions. Lead conversion and all record visibility follow the account's server permissions. Follow-ups are saved through the Opportunity API and synchronized to the responsible user's Calendar when the opportunity is open and has a next action, due time and Calendar access. Activity content loads when opened.

Calls opens the phone keypad directly. Call history is available at the top; missed-call and voicemail badges are shown on their controls. Voicemail number settings are under Settings > Calls. Keypress sounds, navigation performance and incoming-call push handling have been improved.

The AI assistant remains discontinued, as in approved version 6.2.24. This build does not restore AI chat, voice sessions, the assistant button or assistant notification routing.

Use the existing review credentials in App Store Connect. Sign-in provisions the assigned calling line and CRM. There is no native consumer signup or purchase flow.

Chat contains SMS, Team Chat, Social and Live Chat. Settings > Conversations controls visible tabs; account permissions still apply. Social requires a connected provider and an open reply window. Live Chat requires a connected website. Chats retain templates, supported attachments and message actions.

Contacts, Calls, Chat and CRM use the main footer. CRM includes Leads, Clients, Opportunities, Calendar and Tasks, subject to account permissions.
