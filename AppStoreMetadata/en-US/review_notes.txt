Mango9 is a companion application for existing Mango9 business users. Accounts and telecommunications services are provisioned outside the app under organizational service agreements. There is no consumer signup, purchase, pricing, or subscription link in the app.

Sign in using the review credentials entered in App Store Connect. You may also tap “Use an email code” and enter the code delivered to the reviewer email. Both login methods use the same account-provisioning flow.

After login:
1. Confirm the assigned extension shows Connected.
2. Open CRM to view the seeded lead and client.
3. Tap a phone number and select Call with Mango9.
4. Open Conversations, then Team Chat, and use the supplied second reviewer user.
5. In Team Chat, open the safety menu to report or block a user.
6. Send and receive a voice message in SMS or Team Chat to verify the inline audio player.
7. Open About Mango9 > Licensing to view open-source notices and the exact corresponding source for this build: https://github.com/8189164300/Linphone_mango9/tree/ios-6.2.7-build-28

Version 6.2.7 clears SMS and Team Chat unread indicators immediately when a conversation opens and keeps app badge counts synchronized. Selecting a suggested contact also opens its new conversation immediately. Carrier SMS menus now show only supported messaging actions, while muted SMS conversations suppress alerts, display a crossed bell in the list, and retain the correct state after unmuting. Conversation Info now displays server-backed SMS activity, exact CRM phone-number matches, and clearly labeled call history stored on the current iPhone. The update applies consistent Mango9 colors across launch, onboarding, permissions, and sign-in screens.

The review account, review contact details, service endpoints, and safe test number are supplied only in App Store Connect and are not stored in the public source repository.
