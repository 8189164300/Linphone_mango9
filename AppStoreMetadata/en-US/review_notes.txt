Mango9 6.2.28 (81) is an update to the approved 6.2.27 release. Mango9 is a business communications and CRM companion for existing Mango9 customers.

CHANGES IN THIS UPDATE
SMS, Team Chat, connected Social inboxes and website Live Chat now support fast batch actions. Users can select only unread chats, only read chats, or all chats, then mark the selection read, mark it unread or delete it for themselves. The app no longer processes the selection one chat at a time.

The update improves unread indicators, protects messages arriving after a selection is made, and makes retries safe after a connection interruption. Delete for me affects only the signed-in user's history; it does not delete messages for other participants or unsend them on connected social platforms.

HOW TO REVIEW
1. Sign in with the existing email/password credentials in App Review's Sign-In Information.
2. Open Chat and choose SMS, Team Chat, Social or Live Chat.
3. Tap the selection checkmark, then open the selection menu for Select unread, Select read or Select all.
4. Use the read, unread or delete icons. Deletion requires confirmation. Available chats depend on the review account's connected services.

MANUAL SIP ACCESS
Connection settings remain directly available from the gear on the sign-in screen, with no additional settings password. Enter the supplied Extension, SIP password and Server / Domain, then tap Register. The existing private review guide supplies the test calling-line credentials and proxy.mango9.com server.

Manual SIP is a phone-only fallback when CRM/email sign-in or automatic calling-line provisioning is unavailable. It requires SIP credentials supplied by the customer's administrator. Use the normal CRM email/password sign-in to review CRM and messaging.

CRM access, connected providers and calling services follow the signed-in account's assigned permissions. There is no native consumer signup or purchase flow.
