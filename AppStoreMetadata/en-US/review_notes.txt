Mango9 6.2.24 (73): business communications and CRM companion, iOS 15+.

We have decided to discontinue the AI assistant. This update removes its chat and voice features while retaining the app’s other functionality. The assistant button, AI chat screens, voice sessions and assistant notification routing have been removed.

Use the existing review credentials in App Store Connect. Sign-in provisions the assigned calling line and CRM. There is no native consumer signup or purchase flow.

Chat contains SMS, Team Chat, Social and Live Chat. Settings > Conversations controls visible tabs; account permissions still apply. Social requires a connected provider and an open reply window. Live Chat requires a connected website.

Contacts, Calls and CRM use the main footer. CRM includes Leads, Clients, Calendar and Tasks, subject to account permissions. Chats retain templates, media preview/export and message actions.

The sign-in gear opens optional manual SIP setup. Its settings code is the displayed app version, 6.2.24. This opens a form for SIP credentials supplied by an administrator and does not grant CRM permissions. Normal sign-in does not require this setup.
