Mango9 is a companion application for existing Mango9 business users. Accounts and telecommunications services are provisioned outside the app under organizational service agreements. There is no consumer signup, purchase, pricing, or subscription link in the app.

Sign in using the existing review credentials entered in App Store Connect. Password and email-code login use the same account-provisioning flow.

Version 6.2.13 (43) improves New Chat performance for large contact lists, calendar loading and layout, and adds business-hours controls and CRM time-zone synchronization. One binary supports iOS 15 and later, with Day, Week and Month calendar views and the same appointment actions on supported OS versions.

To test:
1. Open CRM > Appointments. Switch between the appointment list and Calendar (Day, Week and Month), and open an appointment to view its status and linked lead or client.
2. Use + to create a test appointment in the review account. Edit its details or status. Sharing and assignment follow the signed-in user's server permissions. Delete only your test appointment afterward.
3. Open Settings > CRM Settings (also available from CRM). Review in-app reminder timing, calendar preferences, and separate SMS and Team Chat notification controls. Appointment reminders appear while the app is open; they are not background push reminders.
4. With microphone permission denied, tap Call. The app explains that the other person cannot hear you and offers Cancel or Open Settings without dialing. If permission has never been requested, iOS asks for access first. Returning from Settings does not automatically place a call.
5. Open and leave SMS conversations repeatedly, view attachments, and verify the existing composer and message actions. Existing Team Chat ordering and media playback remain available.
6. Open Contacts and view a contact. Settings > Contacts shows access controls and a manual reload option; changes to iPhone contacts also refresh automatically. Editing an iPhone contact uses Apple's contact editor. Deleting from that editor removes the contact from iPhone Contacts, so use only a disposable test contact.
7. Open New Chat and scroll or search for a contact. Contact rows load progressively while search covers the full available directory.
8. In Calendar, press and hold a date or empty time slot to start an appointment with that date/time. Use the hours icon to view or edit the review account's business hours and default schedule, subject to account permissions. Closed hours are shaded. CRM time-zone changes sync with the web; personal display-zone preferences remain separate.
9. Open a CRM lead or client. First and last names share a row; City, State and ZIP share a row where space permits. Short fields use compact columns inside section cards; long content stays full-width and accessibility text stacks vertically. The status menu saves just the selected status without entering Edit, subject to the account's field permissions. Edit and Cancel retain existing field permissions and values.
10. The in-app call screen uses a light background, soft gray inactive controls, blue selected controls and red hang-up. Expanded controls, audio output and the in-call keypad use the same palette. Call actions and audio-routing behavior are unchanged.

Appointments use the selected CRM account and its existing permissions; they do not require access to the iPhone's personal calendar.
