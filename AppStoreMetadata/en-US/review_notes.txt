Mango9 is a companion app for existing Mango9 business customers. Accounts and telecommunications services are provisioned outside the app. There is no consumer signup, purchase, pricing or subscription link. Use the review credentials supplied in App Store Connect. Password and email-code login use the same provisioning flow. Version 6.2.20 (60) supports iOS 15 and later.

Review steps:
1. Conversations includes SMS, Team Chat, Social and Live Chat. Open a conversation, rotate the phone and return to the inbox. Settings > Conversations has device-local switches for Team Chat, Social and Live Chat; hiding a tab preserves history and server permissions.
2. In any supported chat, tap + then Templates (fourth item). Personal templates belong to the signed-in account and server permissions control access. Create opens a blank name/message editor with a character counter. Save, open the template, then use Insert or Edit. Insert preserves any current draft and never sends automatically. A read-only account can insert existing templates but cannot modify them. Templates do not bypass channel or provider reply restrictions.
3. Live Chat offers website/status filters, Assigned to me, Unread, device presence, visitor details, transfer and resolve/reopen. Visitor-entered identity is marked unverified; location is approximate. Website settings show server policies and personal notification preferences.
4. The + menu offers supported images and files; voice recording is on the right when supported. Native previews offer sharing/export. Long-press a message for Copy, Select, Forward, Delete for me and supported reactions. Forwarding opens a draft for review. Personal deletion preserves other participants' copies. Retries retain the original send/reaction request.
5. Social replies and media depend on connected-account authorization and provider capabilities. Social outgoing audio and TikTok reactions are unsupported. SMS reactions are staff annotations, not SMS or native tapbacks. Empty accounts remain empty. Loading waits before showing an empty inbox; Social refresh preserves drafts.
6. Tap a message notification to open the matching SMS, Team Chat, Social or Live Chat conversation, including from a closed app. Team Chat supports renaming eligible groups. Personally deleted messages stay hidden from inbox previews; new incoming messages can return a hidden conversation to the inbox.
7. CRM > Calendar has Day/Week/Month views; CRM > Events & Tasks has Appointments, Events and Tasks. Create a test event with +, change its start, save and reopen. Moving the start preserves duration. Recurring editing remains on the web. No personal iPhone calendar access is required.
8. Settings > CRM Settings includes reminders and separate push controls for SMS, Team, Social, leads/imports and Events & Tasks. Task-change pushes are not yet available. In-app reminders are separate from background pushes. Calling uses the assigned business line. Denied microphone permission offers Cancel or Open Settings without dialing.

Voice messages use the media loudspeaker by default and respect connected headphones and active calls. Service notification accounts are excluded from teammate choices. The sign-in screen gear opens manual connection settings; its access code is the app version (6.2.20). This only gates manual settings; normal account sign-in is unchanged and requires no manual server entry.
