Mango9 6.2.26 (78): business communications and CRM companion, iOS 15+.

Build 78 includes fixes for Social Chat attachment and shared-card display, plus platform icons and public profile links in conversation headers. Existing unread and deleted-chat behavior is preserved.

As in approved version 6.2.25, the app includes native CRM Opportunities, including values, stages, details, activity links, account-scoped filters and new-item alerts. Open CRM > Opportunities, or open a Lead or Client and use its Opportunity actions. Lead conversion and all record visibility follow the account's server permissions. Follow-ups are saved through the Opportunity API and synchronized to the responsible user's Calendar when the opportunity is open and has a next action, due time and Calendar access. Activity content loads when opened.

Calls opens the phone keypad directly. Call history is available at the top; missed-call and voicemail badges are shown on their controls. Voicemail number settings are under Settings > Calls. Keypress sounds, navigation performance and incoming-call push handling have been improved.

The AI assistant remains discontinued, as in approved version 6.2.24. This build does not restore AI chat, voice sessions, the assistant button or assistant notification routing.

Use the existing review credentials in App Store Connect. Sign-in provisions the assigned calling line and CRM. There is no native consumer signup or purchase flow.

Chat contains SMS, Team Chat, Social and Live Chat. Settings > Conversations controls visible tabs; account permissions still apply. Social requires a connected provider and an open reply window. Live Chat requires a connected website. Chats retain templates, supported attachments and message actions.

Contacts, Calls, Chat and CRM use the main footer. CRM includes Leads, Clients, Opportunities, Calendar and Tasks, subject to account permissions.

The sign-in gear opens optional manual SIP setup. Its settings code is the displayed app version, 6.2.26. This opens a form for SIP credentials supplied by an administrator and does not grant CRM permissions. Normal sign-in does not require this setup.
