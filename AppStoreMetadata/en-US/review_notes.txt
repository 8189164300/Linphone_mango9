Mango9 is a companion application for existing Mango9 business users. Accounts and telecommunications services are provisioned outside the app under organizational service agreements. There is no consumer signup, purchase, pricing, or subscription link in the app.

Sign in using the existing review credentials entered in App Store Connect. Password and email-code login use the same account-provisioning flow.

Version 6.2.17 (51) brings SMS, Team and Social conversations into one tabbed interface, with consistent attachment and long-press controls. It also separates Calendar from Events & Tasks and adds account-scoped social-message, lead and appointment notifications. One binary supports iOS 15 and later.

To test:
1. Open CRM > Calendar for Day, Week and Month views, or CRM > Events & Tasks for the Appointments, Events and Tasks tabs. The two screens link to each other. Month cells show total event counts. Tap a day to open Day view, then an event for details. Touch and hold a day or time slot to start a new event with that date or time.
2. Use + to create two test events, then change a start date, save and reopen. Moving the start preserves duration; the end is independently editable. Saves are read back from the server before confirmation. Delete only your test events. Recurring schedule editing remains on the web.
3. Open Conversations (also linked from CRM) and switch between SMS, Team and Social. Open an existing message and touch and hold it for supported actions. The gray smiley opens the system emoji keyboard. SMS reactions are shared staff annotations within Mango9: no SMS, native tapback, or text fallback is sent. Facebook/Instagram reactions require a connected, authorized account and current provider capabilities; TikTok reactions are unsupported. An unconfirmed provider command is checked using its saved request rather than blindly resent.
4. The + beside a composer opens supported camera, photo-library and file choices. Social attachment formats and reply availability depend on the provider. Select messages to delete them for yourself across devices or forward available content. Deletion does not unsend messages or remove other staff members' copies. Deleting a conversation hides its existing history for you; a new message can bring it back. Long-press conversation rows for mute and delete controls. A crossed bell indicates mute.
5. Open an eligible Team group and use the pencil to rename it. The server must permit the change; direct conversations have no rename control. In Social, use platform/account filters to narrow existing connected conversations. An account without social authorization shows an access error rather than bypassing authorization.
6. Open Settings > CRM Settings for reminders, calendar preferences and separate push controls for SMS, Team, Social, leads/imports and Events & Tasks. Current Events & Tasks push coverage is appointment booking alerts; task-change pushes are not yet available. In-app reminders remain distinct from background pushes. A lead import produces one summary, not one push per imported lead. Tapping a supported booking or single-lead alert opens that record after authorization.
7. Calling and sign-out behavior are preserved. Denied microphone permission offers Cancel or Open Settings without dialing. Offline sign-out retries call-notification cleanup after connectivity returns, once secure cleanup setup has completed online.

Events use the selected CRM account and its permissions; no personal iPhone calendar access is required.
