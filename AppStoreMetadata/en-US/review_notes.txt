Mango9 is a companion application for existing Mango9 business users. Accounts and telecommunications services are provisioned outside the app under organizational service agreements. There is no consumer signup, purchase, pricing, or subscription link in the app. Sign in using the review credentials entered in App Store Connect. Password and email-code login use the same provisioning flow.

Version 6.2.19 (53) adds Live Chat for authorized website conversations alongside SMS, Team Chat and Social, with visitor details, staff assignment, presence, supported attachments, voice recordings and reactions. It improves chat navigation, portrait/landscape layouts and composer controls. One binary supports iOS 15 and later.

To test:
1. Open Conversations and switch between SMS, Team Chat, Social and Live Chat. Open a saved conversation, rotate the phone and return to the inbox. Settings > Conversations has device-local switches for Team Chat, Social and Live Chat. Hiding a tab preserves history and server permissions.
2. In Live Chat, use website/status filters, Assigned to me and Unread. The presence icon sets this device available or away. Open a conversation and use the information icon for server-supplied visitor context. Visitor-entered identity is explicitly unverified; location is approximate. The conversation menu supports transfer, resolve/reopen and visitor details. Website settings show server policies and personal notification preferences.
3. Live Chat's attachment button is left of the composer and voice recorder is on the right when supported. Native previews provide sharing/export. Long-press a message for Copy, Select, Forward, Delete for me and supported reactions. Selection includes supplied metadata; forwarding opens a draft for review before sending. Personal deletion keeps the visitor's and other staff members' copies. Uncertain sends and reactions retain their original request for retry.
4. SMS reactions are staff annotations within Mango9; no SMS or native tapback is sent. Social replies, media and reactions depend on connected-account authorization and provider capabilities. Social voice recording is not offered because its outgoing API does not support audio. TikTok reactions are unsupported. Empty accounts remain empty.
5. In Team Chat, eligible groups support renaming. Existing SMS, Team and Social history supports personal deletion and mute controls. Deleted content stays hidden from previews. A newly received message can return a previously hidden conversation to the inbox.
6. CRM > Calendar offers Day, Week and Month views; CRM > Events & Tasks offers Appointments, Events and Tasks. Create a test event with +, change its start, save and reopen. Moving the start preserves duration; the end is independently editable. Delete only test events. Recurring schedule editing remains on the web. No personal iPhone calendar access is required.
7. Settings > CRM Settings includes reminders and separate push controls for SMS, Team, Social, leads/imports and Events & Tasks. Task-change pushes are not yet available. In-app reminders are separate from background pushes. Calling remains available through the assigned business line. Denied microphone permission offers Cancel or Open Settings without dialing.
